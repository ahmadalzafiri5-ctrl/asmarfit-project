Support

How can we help?

Write to us at as@development-info.org. We usually answer within two working days. Please tell us your device (iPhone or Android) and, if possible, the e-mail address of your ASFIT account.

Subscription and billing

ASFIT Premium is a subscription (monthly or yearly) that renews automatically until you cancel it. On iPhone you manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

Open the Premium screen in the app, sign in with your ASFIT account and tap Restore purchases.

Apple Health

ASFIT only reads steps, weight, workouts and active energy from Apple Health to show them in the app. You can change this anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → ASFIT. ASFIT never writes data to Apple Health.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app, or follow the steps on this page.

Health notice

ASFIT does not replace medical, dietary or psychological advice. Calorie targets and AI suggestions are estimates — talk to a qualified professional before major changes to your diet or training.
