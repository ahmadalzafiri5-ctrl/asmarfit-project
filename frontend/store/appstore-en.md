**Beschreibung:**

ASFIT is your app for calories, training and real progress. Log meals by photo or barcode, train like in the gym, import recipes straight from a link and ask an AI coach that actually knows your numbers — all in one app you understand at first sight.

YOUR CALORIE COUNTER
• Search foods in any language, scan a barcode or simply photograph your meal — calories and macros are logged for you
• A traffic light for every food: green, yellow, orange by calorie density
• Water, steps and intermittent fasting right on the home screen
• Eating out: the fast-food planner shows the chains available in your country and finds the order with the most protein for your budget

RECIPES THAT FIT YOU
• Recipe import: paste a TikTok, Instagram or website link — ASFIT reads the ingredients and calculates nutrition per serving
• Create recipes with AI, matched to your macros
• Weekly meal plan with a ready-made shopping list

TRAINING LIKE IN THE GYM
• More than 170 exercises with HD demos and an interactive muscle map
• Weekly schedule: choose for each weekday what you train — or rest
• Log sets and weights with one tap and get a suggestion for next time
• Short workouts (7 minutes, HIIT, core, legs) with a real voice
• Workout history, personal records, rest timer, supersets
• Friends duel: share plans and compare your week

YOUR AI COACH
• Knows your goals, meals and workouts — when you allow it
• Answers with concrete numbers: grams, sets, reps
• Builds and saves training plans for you

PROGRESS THAT MOTIVATES
• Weight history, body measurements and a before/after photo comparison
• Phase manager (cut/bulk) with weekly targets
• Recovery score and a weekly auto-review

APPLE HEALTH
• Optionally reads steps, weight, workouts and active energy from Apple Health. ASFIT never writes to Apple Health.
